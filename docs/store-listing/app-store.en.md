### Description  ·  **1711 / 4000** caracteres

JChat connects the people who are in the same place: a bar, a restaurant, a café.

When you arrive at a venue that uses JChat, you join its chat and talk with whoever is there right now. When you leave, the chat stays at the venue.

VENUE CHAT
• Join the chat of the place you're in, with rooms for the venue and for your table.
• We use your location only to confirm you're at the venue. We never show where you are to other people.
• Choose how you want to appear before you join.

MENU AND ORDERS
• Browse the venue's menu on your phone.
• Order from your table and follow your order's status in real time.

OFFERS
• Find the offers and news that businesses near you post.

MATCH
• Meet other people who are at the same venue as you.
• Swipe to like, pass or send a super like. If you both like each other, you can talk.
• Match is optional and is erased when you leave the venue: your likes, matches and Match chats disappear, except with people who follow each other.

TRUTH OR DARE
• Play with the people at the venue using Truth or Dare cards from several categories.
• You decide whether to publish the card you draw or pass.

PRIVATE MESSAGES
• Send a direct message to anyone you like, with photos and voice notes.

YOUR PRIVACY AND SAFETY
• You control who sees your profile and what you share.
• Report or block anyone from a menu, in one tap.
• You can ask the venue staff for help from the app, without anyone else seeing it.
• You can delete your account and your data whenever you want from Settings.

JChat is for people 18 and older only. You can turn off games and Match when you join each venue.

Available in English and Spanish.

Questions or feedback? Write to us from Settings → Contact.
